# Why Clinical Supervision and Professional Judgement Matter

This project is about checking AI explanations. These are the reasons a checked AI answer still cannot replace trained people.

## 1. An AI answer is general. A patient is a specific person.
An AI explanation describes how something usually works. A real patient has a history, a reason for the scan, and risks of their own. Decisions about a scan depend on those details.

## 2. Safety checks follow protocols and trained judgement
Checks such as patient identification, pregnancy status, implants and radiation dose follow local protocols and are done by trained staff. An AI answer does not know the situation in the room.

## 3. Images are interpreted in context
Interpreting an image means combining it with the clinical question and the patient's history. This is done by qualified professionals.

## 4. Students learn under supervision
In my clinical training I observe and assist under supervision. I am a student, and nothing in this repository replaces that supervision or my training.

## 5. When an AI answer and a supervisor disagree
I ask the supervisor, then check a reliable source. I do not choose the AI answer because it sounds confident.

## What this project taught me
- A confident answer can still be wrong.
- Many errors are missing context, not false facts.
- Checking a claim against a source takes time, which is why it is a skill.
