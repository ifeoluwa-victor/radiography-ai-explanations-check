# Practice Examples

Each example is an AI-style statement written for practice. They are not quotes from a real AI product. For each one I name the problem, then write a corrected explanation. The sources I checked are recorded in `verification-log.md`.

---
## Example 1: Hallucination
**AI-style statement:** "Ultrasound uses low-dose X-rays to create images of the baby."

**Problem:** Hallucination. The statement gives the wrong physics.

**Corrected explanation:** Ultrasound uses high-frequency sound waves. It does not use ionizing radiation such as X-rays.

---
## Example 2: Overconfident language
**AI-style statement:** "A pregnancy scan always gives the exact due date, at any stage."

**Problem:** Overconfident. A scan gives an estimate, not an exact date.

**Corrected explanation:** Ultrasound measurements are used to estimate gestational age and the due date. Dating is generally most reliable early in pregnancy and less precise later, so the result is an estimate with some margin.

---
## Example 3: Missing clinical context
**AI-style statement:** "MRI is safe for everyone because it uses no radiation."

**Problem:** Missing context. The statement is half true but leaves out important safety checks.

**Corrected explanation:** MRI does not use ionizing radiation. It uses a strong magnetic field and radio waves. Because of the magnet, patients are screened for implants, devices and metal before a scan, and some implants may not be safe. Safety decisions follow local protocols and trained staff.

---
## Example 4: Unsupported conclusion
**AI-style statement:** "This AI tool detects abnormalities with 99% accuracy, so radiologists are no longer needed."

**Problem:** Unsupported conclusion. The statement gives a number with no source and makes a very large jump.

**Corrected explanation:** A figure like 99% needs a source, and accuracy depends on the tool, the data it was tested on and the setting. A Harvard Medical School study of 140 radiologists found that AI help improved some radiologists' accuracy and worsened others', and that poorly performing AI tools reduced accuracy. AI output therefore needs to be checked by clinicians.

---
## Example 5: Overconfident, missing context
**AI-style statement:** "CT is always better than ultrasound."

**Problem:** Overconfident and missing context.

**Corrected explanation:** The best test depends on the clinical question and the patient. Ultrasound uses no ionizing radiation and is often chosen first for some problems, while CT is better for others. The choice is made by the clinical team following referral guidelines.
